Organization Overview:

Name: SkateMonkey 
SkateMonkey is a non-profit organization founded in 2018 to provide young people with free access to skateboarding as a recreational activity. Established by community volunteers and skateboarding enthusiasts, the organization aims to promote healthy lifestyles, personal development, and social inclusion by offering free skateboarding lessons, training programs, safety workshops, and a dedicated skateboarding playground. Over the years, SkateMonkey has grown through partnerships with schools and local communities, creating a safe and supportive environment where youth can develop their skills, build confidence, and participate in positive recreational activities regardless of their financial background.
Skateboarding is life! And our mission is to empower young people by providing free access to skateboarding facilities, training, and recreational programs that encourage physical fitness, confidence, creativity, and positive community involvement. The vision is to create a future where every young person, regardless of their background, has the opportunity to enjoy skateboarding in a safe and inclusive environment while developing lifelong skills, healthy lifestyles, and strong community connections.
SkateMonkey primarily serves children and young people between the ages of 8 and 21, particularly those from underserved and low-income communities who may have limited access to recreational facilities and sports programs. The organization also welcomes beginner and aspiring skateboarders, local schools, youth groups, and community members who are interested in participating in free skateboarding activities, skills training, mentorship, and community events in a safe and inclusive environment.

1. Index/Home Page
The Index/Home page is the main page of the SkateMonkey website. HTML was used to create the basic structure, including the header, navigation menu, headings, paragraphs, images, lists, and footer. The navigation links allow visitors to move between the Home, About Us, Contact Us, Tickets, and Products pages. CSS was used to create a dark and modern skateboarding design, with a dark header, white text, button-style navigation links, rounded content areas, and shadows. A hover effect was added so navigation buttons change when the user places the mouse over them. The page was also made responsive so it can adjust to smaller screens. 
2. About Us Page
The About Us page provides information about SkateMonkey and its purpose. HTML was used to create sections such as Who We Are, Mission and Vision, What We Do, Our Values, and Our Community. Headings, paragraphs, lists, and separate containers were used to organise the information clearly. CSS was used to give the page a skateboarding-themed background image with an overlay, making the text easier to read. The Mission and Vision sections were placed next to each other using Flexbox on larger screens and are arranged vertically on smaller screens. 
3. Contact Us Page
The Contact Us page allows visitors to communicate with SkateMonkey. HTML was used to create the page heading, contact information, image, and a contact form. The form includes fields for the visitor's name, email address, and message, as well as submit and reset buttons. CSS was used to make the form clean and professional by controlling its width, spacing, borders, padding, and rounded corners. The buttons were also styled and given hover effects. Responsive design allows the contact form to work properly on phones and tablets. 
4. Ticket Sales Page
The Ticket Sales page was created to display SkateMonkey events and collect ticket information from visitors. HTML was used to create an event table containing information such as the event name, date, location, and price. A ticket purchasing form was also included, allowing visitors to enter their information and select tickets or events. CSS was used to make the table organised and easy to read by adding borders, spacing, and padding. The form and buttons use a similar design to the Contact Us page, while responsive styling allows the page to remain usable on smaller screens. 
5. Products Page
The Products page displays SkateMonkey merchandise and includes an order form. HTML was used to create a product table showing the product name, image, description, and price. Products include items such as a SkateMonkey T-shirt, hoodie, cap, and skateboard deck. The order form allows customers to enter their name, email, product, size, quantity, and delivery address. CSS was used to organise the products, control image sizes, add spacing and borders, and make the order form consistent with the other forms on the website. The HTML form only collects the information; a backend system or JavaScript/server-side system would be needed to process actual orders. 
6. Common CSS Design
All five pages use a consistent CSS design so that they appear to be part of the same website. The body styling controls the font, background colour, text colour, spacing, and line height. The header and footer use a dark background with white text, while the navigation links are designed to look like buttons. Hover effects make the navigation more interactive. Content containers use maximum widths, padding, rounded corners, and shadows to make the pages easier to read. A responsive media query was also included so that the website can adjust its layout on smaller screens.
