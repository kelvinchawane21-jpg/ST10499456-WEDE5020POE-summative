Welcome to SkateMonkey

SkateMonkey is a non-profit organisation created to encourage young people and aspiring skaters to participate in skateboarding activities, training and recreational events.

Who We Are

SkateMonkey is a community-focused organisation that aims to make skateboarding accessible to people who are interested in learning, practising and improving their skating skills.

We provide recreational activities, training opportunities and events where members of the community can enjoy skateboarding in a positive environment.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide young and aspiring skateboarders with opportunities to learn, practise and enjoy skateboarding in a safe and supportive community environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build an inclusive skateboarding community where people can develop their skills, meet other skaters and participate in recreational activities.

What We Do

SkateMonkey provides a range of activities and services for skateboard enthusiasts.

  • Skateboarding training and recreational activities
  • Community skateboarding events
  • Event scheduling and ticket sales
  • Skateboarding products and merchandise
  • Opportunities for young people to practise their skills
  • Support for the local skateboarding community

Our Values

  • Community: We encourage people to connect through skateboarding.
  • Inclusivity: We aim to create an environment where everyone can participate.
  • Development: We encourage skaters to learn and develop their skills.
  • Recreation: We promote skateboarding as an enjoyable recreational activity.
  • Opportunity: We aim to provide accessible opportunities for aspiring skateboarders.
